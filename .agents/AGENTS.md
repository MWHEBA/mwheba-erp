# Project Rules for MWHEBA ERP

- **Cross-Template & Module Consistency Directive**: Any UI/UX, styling, layout, formatting, calculation, validation, error handling, or feature modification discussed or requested MUST automatically be audited, propagated, and applied across ALL similar and related templates, forms, detail views, print views, and modules (e.g. Sales Invoices, Purchase Invoices, Quotations, Sales Returns, Purchase Returns, Payment Vouchers, Customer & Supplier detail views) throughout the codebase without leaving any gaps.
